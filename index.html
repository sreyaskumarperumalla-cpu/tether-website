// MECHANICS

HOW TO
SURVIVE

01
PICK YOUR
KEYS

Player 1 moves with WASD. Player 2 moves with IJKL. You are always tethered — neither of you goes anywhere alone.

02
KILL WITH
THE CHAIN

Pass the tether over enemies to destroy them. Your players also fire automatic projectiles — no manual aim required, just stay in position.

03
USE YOUR
ABILITIES

Press certain buttons simultaneously to trigger shared abilities. They draw from a shared energy pool that slowly regenerates — use them wisely.

04
CHASE THE
HIGH SCORE

Every enemy you kill adds to your score. Survive as many waves as possible — the game ends the moment either player loses all their health.

SHARED RESOURCE SYSTEM
❤ PLAYER HEALTH

Each player has their own health pool. If either player reaches zero, the run ends for both — there are no revives. Protect each other at all costs.

⚡ SHARED ENERGY

Abilities consume shared energy. It regenerates over time — coordinate when to use it so you're never caught empty against a big wave.

// THREATS

KNOW YOUR
ENEMIES

Creatures emerge in relentless waves. Each type behaves differently — learn their patterns or get overwhelmed.

  • THE DASHER Lunges at players in sudden bursts. Fast and aggressive — hard to trap with the tether alone. Use abilities or projectiles to intercept mid-dash.
  • THE BRUTE Slow, massive, and built to absorb punishment. Takes multiple tether passes or a barrage of projectiles to bring down. Don't let it corner a player.
  • THE SPITTER Keeps its distance and launches projectiles at both players. Stay mobile and close the gap fast — let it linger and you'll take chip damage all wave.

// ABILITIES

COMBINED
POWERS

When both players press certain buttons at the same time, you trigger a shared ability. These burn shared energy — coordinate or waste them.

SYNC INPUT
CHAIN LASH

Snap the tether outward in a burst, dealing heavy damage to all enemies caught along its length. Best used when you're surrounded.

SYNC INPUT
ENERGY SHIELD

Both players briefly become invulnerable. Use it to survive a Spitter barrage or tank through a Brute when health is low.

SYNC INPUT
OVERDRIVE

Supercharge both players' projectile output for a short window. Shreds Brutes and clears groups of Dashers before they can close in.

⚡ ENERGY REGEN

Shared energy refills automatically over time. You can't rush it — plan your ability usage around wave timing, not desperation.

// THE CHAIN

The chain is your bond — and your weapon. Pass it over enemies to destroy them, sweep it through clusters, or snap it tight to punish anything caught in between.

// PROGRESSION

PERMANENT
UPGRADES

Every few waves, the game pauses and you choose one permanent upgrade. These stack — pick smart and your build compounds over time.

🔗
LONGER TETHER

Permanently increases the maximum distance between players. Cover more ground and trap larger groups of enemies with a single sweep.

💥
MORE DAMAGE

Boosts both tether kill damage and projectile output permanently. Cuts down Brutes faster and melts swarms before they stack up.

❤
FASTER REGEN

Increases the passive health regeneration rate for both players. The longer you survive, the more this compounds — a strong pick in later waves.
