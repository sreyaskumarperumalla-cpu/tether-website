You and your partner are bound by a chain. Move apart to stretch it — move together to let enemies slip past. Distance is your weapon.

Position so the chain catches enemies between you. Contact kills — but only when they're fully ensnared. Coordination is everything.

Enemies come in increasingly brutal waves. Collect power-ups, spend shared gold, and keep your shared health pool above zero.

SHARED RESOURCE SYSTEM
❤ SHARED HEALTH

Both players share one health pool. When it hits zero, the run ends for both — no solo saves.

◆ SHARED GOLD

Kills award gold to the shared pool. Either player can spend it — trust your partner.

// THREATS

KNOW YOUR
ENEMIES

Creatures from the deep emerge in relentless waves. Each type has a unique movement pattern — learn them or perish.

  • THE SHAMBLER Slow, predictable. Moves in straight lines toward the nearest player. Easy to trap — watch for groups.
  • THE DARTER Fast and erratic. Changes direction unpredictably. Requires both players to anticipate its path.
  • THE BEHEMOTH Large and armored. Takes multiple chain wraps to eliminate. Tanks through careless formations.
  • THE SWARM Dozens of tiny enemies moving in formation. Chain must be swept through the group rapidly.
